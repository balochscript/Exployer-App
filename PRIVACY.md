# Privacy — Exployer

**Short version: your files never leave your phone. There is no account and no sign-in.**

## What stays on the device

- Files and folders you open, move, rename, compress, encrypt or delete
- Playback history, queues, tags and the recycle bin
- The password vault (AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2-derived keys) — the master password is never stored
  anywhere else and cannot be recovered by us
- Browser history, bookmarks, saved logins and site permissions, unless you enable clear-on-exit

None of this is uploaded, synced or backed up by Exployer.

## Permissions and what they are for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| All files access | Managing files and folders is the core of the app |
| Camera, microphone, location | Only handed to a site you opened, and only after you allow it for that site. You can deny, allow once, or remember the choice per site |
| Notifications | Playback controls and download progress |
| Install unknown apps (optional) | Only if you choose to install an APK from inside the file manager |

## Ads

Exployer is free and supported by advertising delivered by a third-party ad network (Tapsell). That
network may collect device and advertising identifiers as described in its own privacy policy; it does
not receive your files, your history or the contents of your vault. Watching rewarded videos removes
ads for seven days.

## Network use

The browser and the video downloader are the only parts that talk to the internet, and only with
addresses you open or request. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
